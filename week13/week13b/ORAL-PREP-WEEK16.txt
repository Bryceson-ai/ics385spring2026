------------------------------------------------------------

PART 4 — WHAT MAKES A STRONG ORAL OPENING
------------------------------------------------------------
The instructor is NOT testing whether you memorized the slides.
They are testing whether you actually made the decisions yourself.

Strong answers:
  "I chose a dropdown because on mobile at 375px, tabs would have
   collapsed awkwardly and I already had a select element in IslandList."

Weak answers:
  "I chose a dropdown because it was the best option for UX."

Strong answers:
  "I used Kihei because my flagship property — Kihei Studio by the Sea —
   is literally in Kihei, so the weather there is the most relevant."

Weak answers:
  "I used Kihei because it is a city on Maui."

The difference: specificity. Tie every answer back to YOUR property,
YOUR visitor segment, YOUR actual code decisions.
