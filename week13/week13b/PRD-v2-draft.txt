SECTION 4: DASHBOARD DESIGN (NEW)

Three Charts — with rationale for the Canadian winter vacationer segment:

--- Chart 1: Average Nightly Rate by Property (Bar Chart) ---
Data source: MongoDB properties collection, nightlyRate field.
Chart type: Bar chart (Chart.js Bar).
Why it matters for the segment: Canadian travelers are sensitive to
exchange rate fluctuations — seeing prices side by side lets them compare
value quickly without clicking into each listing. A bar chart communicates
relative cost more clearly than a table for non-technical users.

--- Chart 2: Average Stay Duration by Visitor Segment (Horizontal Bar) ---
Data source: DBEDT Hawaii Tourism Authority visitor data CSV,
fields: VisitorSegment, AvgNightsStayed.
Chart type: Horizontal bar (Chart.js Bar with indexAxis: 'y').
Why it matters for the segment: The operator needs to see whether Canadian
vacationers have meaningfully longer stays than other segments so they can
price minimum-night requirements correctly. This chart answers that question
directly.

--- Chart 3: Visitor Arrivals by Month (Line Chart) ---
Data source: DBEDT monthly visitor arrivals CSV,
fields: Month, TotalArrivals, CanadaArrivals.
Chart type: Line chart (Chart.js Line).
Why it matters for the segment: Canadian bookings spike November–March
(winter escape season). The line chart shows the operator when to raise
minimum stays and lets visitors understand when Maui is most crowded.

SECTION 7: DATA ARCHITECTURE (NEW)

DBEDT Data Source:
Hawaii Tourism Authority publishes monthly and annual visitor statistics
at dbedt.hawaii.gov. The relevant CSV for this project is the Annual
Visitor Research Report, specifically:

  Fields used:
  - VisitorOrigin (filter to "Canada")
  - AverageNightsStayed
  - TotalArrivals
  - Month (for time-series chart)
  - IslandVisited (filter to "Maui")

JSON schema (converted from CSV):
[
  {
    "month": "January",
    "year": 2024,
    "island": "Maui",
    "origin": "Canada",
    "totalArrivals": 12400,
    "avgNightsStayed": 7.2
  },
  ...
]

Express route that serves the data:
  GET /api/stats/visitors
  - Optional query params: ?island=Maui&origin=Canada&year=2024
  - Returns the JSON array above filtered by params
  - Data is seeded from the converted CSV via a seed script in scripts/
